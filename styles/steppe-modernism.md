## Style block

clean architectural visualisation render, sculptural Soviet-modernist concrete forms, raw board-marked concrete with sun-bleached pastel paint in faded turquoise, salmon pink and mustard yellow, chipped tile mosaic murals of heroic workers and radiant suns, flat pale hazy daylight with soft shadows, a vast deserted space with wide negative space, centred frontal composition, still, quiet and desolate

## Intent

Inspired by the bus stops of the former Soviet Union: one bold, slightly absurd concrete
form standing alone in a lot of empty space. Desolate rather than grimy. The paint is faded
by the sun, not dirty, and the light is flat and quiet. Rendered as a clean architectural
visualisation, as if each place were a modernist competition entry that got built and then
forgotten.

The satire comes from the gap between grand, hopeful civic architecture and what it is for.
The corporation's prison, yard and scrapyard are monuments nobody visits, and the mosaics
still promise freedom over an empty floor.
