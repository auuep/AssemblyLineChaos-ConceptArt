# The factory — grimy cinematic

- **Subject:** location, the factory
- **Framing:** wide establishing (three variations: from the line's far end, from the catwalk office, from the floor by the build bay)
- **Style block:** loose cinematic digital oil painting with visible brushstrokes, harsh flickering overhead fluorescent light, hazy air thick with drifting dust, oily wet concrete reflections, deep crushed shadows, desaturated industrial palette of grey, muted green and sickly yellow, the atmosphere of a bleak film still

## A — from the far end of the line (16:9)

```text
A loose cinematic digital oil painting of a vast, empty factory-prison assembly hall, seen in a wide establishing shot from the far end of the assembly line. A long conveyor runs straight down the centre of the hall toward a huge open vehicle door at the opposite end, where pale daylight spills in across the floor. Halfway along, a half-built car sits in a single build bay with parts lying beside it. Tall metal shelving stacked with car parts lines both side walls. High above the line, a glass-windowed office sits on a steel catwalk, its lights on, looking down over the floor. A faded banner hangs from the rafters reading "PRODUCTIVITY IS REHABILITATION". Visible brushstrokes, harsh flickering overhead fluorescent light, hazy air thick with drifting dust, oily wet concrete reflections, deep crushed shadows, desaturated industrial palette of grey, muted green and sickly yellow, the atmosphere of a bleak film still, strong one-point perspective.
```

## B — from the catwalk office (16:9)

```text
A loose cinematic digital oil painting looking down over a factory-prison assembly hall from behind the glass of a catwalk office high above the floor, a wide establishing shot. The edge of a cluttered desk and the window frame sit in the dark foreground, smeared with reflections. Below, the assembly line cuts diagonally across the hall toward a large open vehicle door, with a half-built car waiting in its build bay. Rows of metal shelving full of car parts line the walls, and a few tiny, featureless worker silhouettes stand small and isolated on the concrete floor. In the far corner, a vending machine glows beside a prize wheel. Visible brushstrokes, harsh flickering overhead fluorescent light, hazy air thick with drifting dust, oily wet concrete reflections, deep crushed shadows, desaturated industrial palette of grey, muted green and sickly yellow, the atmosphere of a bleak film still, high-angle perspective emphasising scale and surveillance.
```

## C — from the floor by the build bay (21:9 or 16:9)

```text
A loose cinematic digital oil painting of a factory-prison assembly hall in a low, wide establishing shot taken from floor level beside the build bay. In the foreground, a half-assembled car chassis sits on the line, with a heavy engine hanging from a chain hoist above it, slightly askew. The assembly line stretches away to the right toward a towering open vehicle door full of hazy daylight. On the left, tall metal shelves of car parts recede into shadow. Above, a glass office on a steel catwalk catches the light, and a wall sign reads "DAYS WITHOUT INCIDENT: 0". Visible brushstrokes, harsh flickering overhead fluorescent light, hazy air thick with drifting dust, oily wet concrete reflections, deep crushed shadows, desaturated industrial palette of grey, muted green and sickly yellow, the atmosphere of a bleak film still, low camera angle making the hall feel enormous.
```
