# Soviet prototype cars — steppe modernism

Three small Soviet cars from the 1950s–80s that never reached mass production, each shown alone
in the steppe-modernism world from three angles. The vehicles are described by shape as well as
name, since Krea is unlikely to know the models.

- **Subject:** vehicles. A = NAMI-050 "Belka" microcar (1955), B = VNIITE-PT taxi (1964),
  C = "Pangolina" homemade sports car (1980)
- **Framing:** per vehicle, one low three-quarter or head-on hero shot, one wide or side view
  and one unusual angle (overhead, interior or elevated)
- **Style block:** clean architectural visualisation render, sculptural Soviet-modernist concrete forms, raw board-marked concrete with sun-bleached pastel paint in faded turquoise, salmon pink and mustard yellow, chipped tile mosaic murals of heroic workers and radiant suns, flat pale hazy daylight with soft shadows, a vast deserted space with wide negative space, centred frontal composition, still, quiet and desolate
